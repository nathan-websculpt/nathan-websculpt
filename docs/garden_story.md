## Garden Sim — Background

Garden Sim started as a way to explore C++ systems, but became a combination of two interests:

- simulation systems
- horticulture / botany

---

### The Idea

Instead of building a typical farming game, the focus was:

- treating flowers as meaningful systems
- modeling growth and interaction
- tying mechanics to real-world inspiration

---

### Core Concept

Flowers influence **Garden Morale**, which:

- unlocks new actions
- affects progression
- ties aesthetic elements to gameplay systems

---

### Systems in the Game

- NPC helpers
- fertilizer affecting yields
- water management
- beekeeping
- fast travel
- buying/selling systems

---

### Why Gardening?

Horticulture provides:

- layered systems (soil, water, growth, environment)
- long-term state evolution
- natural constraints

This maps well to simulation design.

---

### Development Approach

- built without a game engine
- deterministic simulation model
- systems-first design

### Built by a Gardener — Some of my Favorite Plants

#### **🌱 Cranberry Hibiscus** *(Hibiscus acetosella 'Mahogany Splendor')*

- Grows up to 7-8 feet
- Blooms in the fall
- An affordable alternative to Japanese Maples (the foliage is close enough)
- Great for end-of-season chop-n-drop

<img src="/docs/assets/images/hibiscus_cranberry.jpg" alt="Cranberry Hibiscus" width="60%">

####  **🌺 Tropical Hibiscus** *(Hibiscus rosa-sinensis)*

- Specific soil and nutrient requirements
- Loves water, but doesn't like staying wet
- Vibrant and showy all season long
- I study the hybridization of these

<img src="/docs/assets/images/hibiscus_tropical.jpg" alt="Tropical Hibiscus">

####  **🌼 Cosmos** *(Cosmos Sulphureus)*

- Self-seeding annual
- Blooms all season
- Drought-tolerant once established
- Be careful that they don't take over your yard!

<img src="/docs/assets/images/cosmos.jpg" alt="Cosmos" width="60%">

#### **🌸 Morning Glory** *(over 1,000 species across several genera within the Convolvulaceae family, the most common being Ipomoea, Convolvulus, Calystegia, Merremia)*

- Vining
- Blooms in the morning
- Likely to be invasive in your area
- You might have to battle rust all season long, but it is worth it

<img src="/docs/assets/images/morning_glory.jpg" alt="Morning Glory" width="60%">
